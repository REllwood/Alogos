## Robustness to edits

A model trained without the Defactify test split, applied to 1,200 of its images before
and after each edit. "Flips" is the share of verdicts that change.

| Edit | Real flagged as AI | AI flagged as AI | Flips |
|---|---|---|---|
| None | 33% | 61% | 0% |
| Re-save as JPEG quality 95 | 33% | 61% | 1% |
| Re-save as JPEG quality 50 | 8% | 31% | 31% |
| Resize to half size | 54% | 77% | 24% |
| Crop 3 px (moves the JPEG grid) | 25% | 45% | 15% |

## Excluding JPEG block boundaries

Dropping every gradient that straddles an 8 × 8 JPEG block boundary did not improve
accuracy, and made verdicts much less stable when an image is cropped (which moves the
JPEG grid): 22% of verdicts flipped after a 3-pixel crop, against 15%
without the exclusion. The detector therefore does not use it.
